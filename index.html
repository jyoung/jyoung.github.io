About Me

My career in software development began at the dawn of interactive web development—before AJAX was coined and jQuery existed. Back then, we built dynamic experiences with plain JavaScript and remote scripting techniques, pushing the boundaries of what browsers could do. Today, I'm leveraging AI agents across the entire software development lifecycle, from design and coding to testing and deployment.

What sets my perspective apart is extensive experience with low-code and model-driven platforms. I cut my teeth on COOL:Gen building enterprise middleware, spent years with Visual LANSA modernizing IBM iSeries systems, and now lead a team delivering integration solutions with MuleSoft. These tools taught me that the best solution isn't always the most technically elegant—it's the one that solves the problem efficiently and can be maintained by the team you have.

This breadth of experience has made me pragmatic rather than dogmatic about technology choices. I've seen paradigms come and go, watched "best practices" evolve, and learned that context matters more than convention. Whether it's hand-crafted code or a low-code platform, the right tool depends on the problem, the team, and the constraints you're working within.

Throughout my career, I've worn many hats—developer, architect, team lead, and mentor. What I enjoy most is creating tools that make users' jobs easier and helping teams navigate the ever-changing technology landscape with clarity and purpose.
